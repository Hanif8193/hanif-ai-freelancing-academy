# Hanif AI Freelancing Academy

**Learn. Build. Freelance with AI.**

Welcome to Hanif AI Freelancing Academy — your comprehensive guide to building a successful freelancing career with the power of AI.

## What is This Academy?

Hanif AI Freelancing Academy is an interactive, AI-powered freelancing education platform that teaches you freelancing from beginner to advanced level. Whether you're completely new to freelancing or an experienced developer looking to leverage AI, this academy has something for you.

## What You'll Learn

- **Freelancing Fundamentals** - Master the basics of freelancing
- **AI-Powered Development** - Learn to use AI coding agents and tools
- **Spec-Driven Development** - Adopt professional development methodologies
- **Real-World Projects** - Build portfolio pieces and gain practical experience

## Who Is This For?

- Complete beginners new to freelancing and tech
- Freelancers wanting to improve their skills
- Junior developers starting their careers
- Full-stack developers exploring freelancing
- AI developers wanting to leverage AI in freelancing
- Urdu-speaking learners who prefer learning in their language

## Learning Paths

1. **Beginner** → Start from zero knowledge
2. **AI-Assisted Developer** → Learn to use AI tools
3. **Spec-Driven Developer** → Master professional methodologies
4. **AI Agent Developer** → Build AI-powered applications
5. **Advanced Freelancer** → Scale your freelancing business
6. **AI-Powered Freelancer** → Leverage AI for maximum productivity

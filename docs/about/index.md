# About Hanif AI Freelancing Academy

Learn about our mission, vision, and the team behind the academy.

## Our Mission

To empower individuals with the skills and knowledge to succeed in freelancing through AI-powered education.

## Our Vision

To become the leading platform for AI-powered freelancing education, bridging the gap between traditional learning and modern freelancing demands.

## The Platform

Hanif AI Freelancing Academy is an interactive, AI-powered freelancing education platform that teaches users freelancing from beginner to advanced level through:

- Interactive eBook content
- Real-world projects
- AI coding agents
- Spec-Driven Development
- MCP (Model Context Protocol)
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
- Hanif AI Tutor
- English ↔ Urdu learning support

## Future Features

We're continuously building new features to enhance your learning experience:

- **Ask the Book / RAG** - Get answers from our content
- **Hanif AI Tutor** - Personalized AI tutoring
- **English ↔ Urdu Translator** - Learn in your language
- **MCP Labs** - Hands-on AI agent development
- **AI Freelancing Agents** - Automate your workflow
- **Courses** - Structured learning paths
- **Community** - Connect with other freelancers
- **Mentorship** - Get guidance from experts
- **Certification** - Validate your skills
